Our story

Named for Goose

Rachel and Richard Targett opened Loose Goose in December 2021 on Terre Haute's south side, at 3020 S. 7th Street. They named it for Goose, their black Labrador Retriever.

Rachel had worked as a barista and a retail manager. She wanted control over quality and customer service, so she opened her own shop, with help from Crimson Cup's startup program.

“Nothing is better than coffee with your best friend,” Rachel said. “Don't forget to bring your pups through the drive-thru!”

In December 2024 a second shop opened on Lafayette Avenue, on the north side. It is drive-thru only.
