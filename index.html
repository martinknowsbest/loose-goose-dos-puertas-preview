The story

Rachel and Richard Targett opened Loose Goose in December 2021 on Terre Haute's south side, at 3020 S. 7th Street.

Rachel had worked as a barista and a retail manager. She opened her own shop to gain control over quality and customer service. Crimson Cup's 7 Steps to Success program helped them get started.

“Nothing is better than coffee with your best friend,” Rachel said. “Don't forget to bring your pups through the drive-thru!”

The shop is named for Goose, the Targetts' black Labrador. In December 2024 a second location opened on Lafayette Avenue, on the north side. It is drive-thru only.

“We wanted to expand to the northside and give northsiders a good local coffee shop to come to,” said manager Lydia Neidermeier.
